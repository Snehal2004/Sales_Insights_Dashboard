# 📊 Sales Insights Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Sales Insights Dashboard** is a business intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze sales performance and revenue trends. It provides an interactive view of revenue, sales quantity, market performance, monthly revenue trends, top customers, and top-performing products.

The dashboard helps transform raw sales transaction data into meaningful visual insights that can support business analysis and strategic decision-making.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Monitor overall revenue and sales quantity.
- Analyze monthly revenue trends over time.
- Compare revenue generated across different markets.
- Understand sales quantity distribution by market.
- Identify the top five customers by revenue.
- Identify the top five products by revenue.
- Explore sales performance using year and month filters.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI** — Dashboard development and visualization
- **Power Query** — Data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX** — Measures and KPI calculations
- **Data Modeling** — Working with related sales tables

## 📂 Dataset & Data Model

The project uses a relational sales data model with tables covering:

- Sales transactions
- Customer information
- Product details
- Market information
- Date and calendar information
- Profit targets
- Base measures

These tables support the analysis of revenue, sales quantity, customer performance, product performance, and market trends.

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **Revenue:** Displays total revenue generated.
- **Sales Quantity:** Shows the total quantity of products sold.

### 2. Revenue Trend
Visualizes monthly revenue changes over time to identify growth patterns and declines.

### 3. Revenue by Markets
Compares revenue across markets to identify the locations contributing most to overall sales.

### 4. Sales Quantity by Markets
Displays sales quantities across different markets for market-level performance analysis.

### 5. Top 5 Customers
Highlights the five customers generating the highest revenue.

### 6. Top 5 Products
Identifies the products contributing the most revenue.

### 7. Interactive Time Filters
Allows users to filter the dashboard by year and month to explore sales performance across different periods.
